Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal in Agra is a famous white marble monument and UNESCO World Heritage Site.

2. Golden Temple

The Golden Temple in Amritsar is an important Sikh place of worship.

3. Red Fort

The Red Fort in Delhi is a historic monument associated with India's Mughal period.

4. Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal in Jaipur is famous for its distinctive pink façade and many windows.

5. Himalayan Mountains

The Himalayas offer spectacular mountain landscapes and opportunities for adventure.
