5. College Terminology

Lecture
A class in which a teacher explains a topic to students.
Assignment
A task given to students to practice and understand a subject.
Semester
One of the academic periods into which a college year is divided.
Attendance
The record of a student's presence in classes.
Examination
A test used to evaluate a student's knowledge and understanding.
